# Fetal Kick

## Fetal Kick

Fetal Kick helps you count and track fetal movement sessions.

It is built for parents who want a clear and simple experience.

### Best for

* Kick counting
* Routine session tracking
* Keeping logs easy to follow

### What to expect

* A focused counter experience
* Simple session tracking
* Clearer monitoring over time

{% hint style="info" %}
Fetal Kick supports tracking and record-keeping. It does not replace professional medical guidance.
{% endhint %}
