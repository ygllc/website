# Apps

## Apps

YG LLC builds practical apps for everyday tracking.

Each product stays focused on one job and keeps the experience simple.

### MyMileage

**Your smart vehicle mileage tracker.**

MyMileage helps drivers keep mileage records organized and easier to review.

It fits people who want a cleaner way to track trips and vehicle usage.

* Track mileage in one place
* Keep records easier to review
* Stay organized with less friction

### Fetal Kick

**The next-gen kick counter.**

Fetal Kick gives parents a calm, modern way to count and track kick sessions.

It is built for people who want a clearer and simpler tracking experience.

* Count kicks with less friction
* Keep sessions clear and consistent
* Use a focused, easy-to-follow experience
